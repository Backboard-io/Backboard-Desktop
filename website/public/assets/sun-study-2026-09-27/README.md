# Kevin — Sun studies

Ten original assets generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the Kevin design direction. This pack is separate from the main page; no integration or deployment has been performed.

## Composition

Chalk-white sculptural acrylic texture, warm sunlight, saffron and vermilion paint, occasional miniature figures. Most images use a wide 3:1 composition for section transitions.

## Responsive usage

- **Abstract strips (03, 05, 09, 10):** appropriate for horizontal stretching. Texture will elongate intentionally; avoid extreme vertical compression.
- **Scenes (01, 02, 04, 06, 07):** use `width: 100%; height: auto` or crop with `object-fit: cover`. Do not stretch people, courts or sun discs.
- **Solar disc (08):** keep a square aspect ratio and place as a separate overlay.
- **Transparent overlays (08–10):** preserve PNG alpha. Test over both the white canvas and orange sections.
- On phones, use a shorter crop or retain the full panoramic strip; keep essential subjects away from clipped edges.
- These are original non-tileable images, not seamless textures. Do not repeat them as tiled backgrounds.

Full generation prompts and asset notes are recorded in `prompts.json`. Open `index.html` for the visual catalogue.
