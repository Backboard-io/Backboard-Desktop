FAQs

VIEW DOCUMENTATION ↗
Is Backboard Desktop free?

The desktop source is open source under the MIT license. Agent providers and hosted services have their own accounts, credentials, and pricing.

Which agents can I use?

Backboard Desktop includes integrations for Backboard, Claude, Codex, and Copilot. Availability depends on your provider setup and authentication.

What are the three workspace modes?

Simple focuses on agent conversations and reviewing changes. Editor brings files, terminals, debugging, and extensions together. Advanced helps you supervise concurrent sessions in grid or kanban views.

Can I use my existing tools?

Backboard builds on Code – OSS and includes an editor, terminal, browser, and extension support. Skills and MCP integrations let you extend agent workflows with your own instructions and tools.

How do I get started?

Clone the repository and follow the setup guide for your platform. Build and launch the app, connect your chosen provider, and start a session with your project.
