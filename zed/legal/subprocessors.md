# Backboard Studio Alpha Subprocessors

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Backboard Studio is in alpha. This temporary list identifies categories of third-party services that may process data while operating the alpha. The official subprocessor list will be published before general availability.

Potential subprocessor categories include:

- Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
- Database, storage, logging, and monitoring providers
- Authentication, security, and abuse-prevention providers
- AI model providers used to process prompts, context, files, or generated outputs
- Customer support, analytics, and product operations tools

If you connect your own model provider, API key, external agent, or integration, that service is controlled by you and may process data under its own terms.

This temporary list is not final and may change during alpha.
