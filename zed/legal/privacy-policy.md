# Backboard Studio Alpha Privacy Notice

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Backboard Studio is in alpha. This temporary privacy notice describes the expected handling of data during early testing and will be replaced with an official privacy policy before general availability.

Backboard Studio may process information you provide to the product, including prompts, chat history, workspace metadata, selected files, generated outputs, diagnostics, logs, API-key configuration state, and usage events needed to operate and improve the alpha.

Backboard Studio may send prompts, selected project context, and generated outputs to Backboard services and configured model providers when you use AI features. If you configure third-party providers or external agents, those providers may process data under their own terms and policies.

We use alpha data to operate the service, debug issues, improve reliability, detect abuse, enforce limits, and understand product usage. We do not intend this alpha build for regulated, highly sensitive, or production-secret workflows.

Do not enter sensitive personal data, production credentials, confidential third-party material, or regulated data unless you have permission and accept the risk of using alpha software.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate for an alpha product, but no system is perfectly secure. If you believe your API key or other secret has been exposed, revoke it immediately.

This notice is temporary and does not replace the final Backboard Studio privacy policy.
