# Cats

Cats are graceful, curious companions known for their independence and quiet charm. Whether chasing a toy or napping in a sunbeam, they bring calm and character to a home.

## Traits

- **Independent** — they enjoy affection on their own terms
- **Curious** — boxes, windowsills, and new sounds draw them in
- **Agile** — soft landings and clever climbs come naturally
- **Expressive** — purrs, chirps, and slow blinks speak volumes

## Why people love them

Cats make great company without demanding constant attention. A gentle head bump, a warm lap nap, or playful pounces turn simple moments into something special. Their mix of mystery and affection wins people over worldwide.
