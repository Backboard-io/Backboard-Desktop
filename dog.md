# Dogs

Dogs are loyal companions who have shared life with people for thousands of years. Playful, affectionate, and eager to please, they fit into homes of almost every kind.

## Traits

- **Loyal** — they form strong bonds and stick by their people
- **Social** — many dogs love company, walks, and play
- **Expressive** — tail wags, ears, and eyes say a lot
- **Trainable** — they learn routines, tricks, and good manners

## Why people love them

Dogs offer friendship without judgment. A walk, a game of fetch, or quiet time on the couch can brighten an ordinary day. Their energy and warmth make them family for millions of people around the world.
